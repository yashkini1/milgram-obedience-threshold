What is being computed? Rather than displaying only the final 65% result, this program reconstructs the changing population of obedient participants at every 15-V step. Each participant is assigned the refusal threshold reported in Milgram's data. The program then calculates the number remaining, refusal rate, obedience percentage, and survival curve as apparent harm increases.
